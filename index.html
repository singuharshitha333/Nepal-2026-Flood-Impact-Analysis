01 / THE EVENT

A disaster measured in more than numbers.

The 25–26 August 2026 flash-flood and mass-movement event caused severe damage in Rasuwa District. This project focuses specifically on AOI01 Syapru Besi and translates the mapped damage assessment into an accessible analytics story.

The underlying EMSR927 grading products were produced from post-event satellite imagery and photo-interpretation. The assessment is rapid and imagery-based, so it should not be treated as a complete ground-survey inventory.

Source: Copernicus Emergency Management Service (EMSR927), accessed through UNESCO IHP-WINS.

03 / ANALYSIS

What the data tells us

The assessment shows substantial impact across buildings, transportation, land use and critical infrastructure within the 346-hectare assessment area.

🏠 Building impact

Across the AOI, 433 of 559 mapped buildings were classified as affected. Residential buildings account for 392 affected buildings, while 41 of 42 mapped non-residential buildings were affected.

323 Destroyed32 Damaged78 Possibly damaged126 No visible damage

🏢 Residential vs non-residential

The affected share differs substantially between asset groups: 392/517 residential buildings and 41/42 non-residential buildings were classified as affected.

517 Residential AOI392 Residential affected42 Non-residential AOI41 Non-residential affected

🛣️ Transportation impact

The assessment recorded 5.4 km of primary roads, 1.1 km of local roads, 1.1 km of cart tracks and 5 bridges/elevated highways affected. The helipad assessment covered 0.01 ha.

🌳 Land-use impact

A total of 111 hectares of represented land-use area was affected across forests, shrub/herbaceous areas, inland wetlands, other land and heterogeneous agricultural land.
